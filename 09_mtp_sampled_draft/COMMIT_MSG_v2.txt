perf(mtp): sample the speculative draft instead of taking its argmax

The proposal head proposes greedily, so its distribution q is a single point and the acceptance
rule degenerates into "accept with probability p(x*)" under the truncated target. Drawing the
draft from the head's own truncated distribution makes the expected acceptance sum_x min(p, q)
instead of E[p(argmax q)], which is larger whenever the target keeps useful mass outside its mode.

The sampler returns the probability of the chosen token together with its whole truncated support,
the acceptance kernel applies min(1, p/q), and a rejection resamples from the normalised residual
(p - q)+. Both schemes are exact speculative sampling: the output distribution equals the truncated
target before and after, only the fraction of accepted drafts moves. With a one-point q every
formula collapses to the previous one, so under --greedy the result has to match bit for bit, and
it does.

The q buffer lives in device rows while drafts travel through the host ingress and egress, and
rows are reassigned when a request finishes. The token q was recorded for is therefore stored next
to it: on a mismatch the position is rejected and the replacement is drawn from the full truncated
p, which is an ordinary non-speculative step. The distribution is preserved exactly; the cost is
one lost speculation per change of tenant.

Measured on RTX 5090, sm_120a, CUDA 13.1.115, qwen3_8_27b_nvfp4, KV int8, MTP3 with
--lm-head-draft, eight prose prompts of 256 new tokens each.

  arm            rounds  drafted  accepted  acceptance  tokens/round  tok/s
  master            861     2571      1175      45.70%         2.365  145.27
  this, off         861     2571      1175      45.70%         2.365  142.47
  this, on          727     2172      1153      53.08%         2.586  158.99

The off arm reproduces master to the token, which is the control that matters: an earlier
implementation passed the q pointer unconditionally while filling it only under the flag, so the
disabled arm read zeros, u*q < p degenerated into p > 0, and the baseline reported 88.28%
acceptance instead of 45.70%. The pointer is now bound under the same condition as the fill.

Under --greedy all three arms agree to the token (729 rounds, 2177 drafted, 1240 accepted,
56.96%), and greedy generation over three ~15K-token prompts matches master byte for byte.

ctest is 97 of 98; ninfer_qwen3_6_frontend_test fails on clean master too.
