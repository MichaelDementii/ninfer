perf(nvfp4): make the TMA route read activation scales tile-contiguous

The activation scale descriptor is encoded as a 16-byte box over the scale plane, so one stage of
the TMA pipeline asks for BlockM separate 16-byte transfers to deliver 4 KiB. The codes next to it
travel as one 64-byte box per row and cost nothing like that.

The cost is measurable in isolation. A producer-only kernel with the same four descriptors, the
same 29 696-byte stage and the same three-deep mbarrier ring, with no math at all, takes 575.9 us
where the real kernel takes 712.7 us: the supply alone is 81% of the kernel. Taking that stage
apart, codes alone move at 6977 GB/s; adding the weight scales costs 14.8% of the time for 4.2%
more bytes; adding the activation scales costs 73.0% of the time for 16.7% more bytes.

Writing the same bytes tile-contiguous - one [BlockM tokens, 16 groups] tile per request - restores
the codes-only rate: 356.0 us against 527.4 us for the same payload, 7009 GB/s against 4732. The
byte order inside a tile is unchanged, so the shared image the consumer reads is identical and the
consumer is untouched.

The quantizer therefore writes the scale plane blocked when the GEMM that follows will take the TMA
route, and keeps the row-major plane otherwise: the same buffer is read by the small-T and GEMV
routes. The predicate is the route's own, and the fused SwiGLU route admits every multiple of 256
from 256 up, which is wider than the shared one.

Measured on RTX 5090, sm_120a, CUDA 13.1.115, qwen3_8_27b_nvfp4, KV int8, prefill chunk 4096.
Operator at T=4096: linear_add [5120,17408] 712.7 -> 555.0 us (0.779), linear_add [5120,6144]
256.0 -> 210.9 (0.824), fused SwiGLU [34816,5120] 1411.1 -> 1187.8 (0.842). Against a measured
feedable ceiling of 1886 TOP/s for this MMA in the kernel's own 4x8 tile ratio, that moves the
route from 54% to 70% of what the hardware can be fed. End-to-end prefill of 32768 tokens
4214.5 -> 4096.8 ms (-2.79%), 7775 -> 7999 tok/s. Decode is untouched at 16.4553 -> 16.4249 ms per
round: its token counts never reach the tile.

Values are unchanged - only the address the scale byte is written to moves - and greedy generation
over four ~15K-token prompts matches the previous binary byte for byte.

ctest is 97 of 98; ninfer_qwen3_6_frontend_test fails on clean master too.
