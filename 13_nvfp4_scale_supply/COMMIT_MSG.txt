perf(nvfp4): fetch exactly the scale bytes a stage consumes

The TMA route's scale traffic did not match what the stage reads.

Activation scales: the descriptor described a [BlockM tokens, 16 groups] tile, but a K128
stage consumes eight of the sixteen group bytes per token. The producer therefore fetched
every tile twice -- once for the even k-tile and once for the odd one -- and the consumer
picked its half with a (k_tile & 1) selector. An eight-group tile is exactly one stage's
worth, so the second fetch and the selector both go away. The stage drops from 29696 to
27648 bytes, and the quantizer writes the same scale byte at the matching offset.

Weight scales: one stage's weight scales are already a contiguous 1024-byte run, but the
box described it as 16 bytes by 64 rows, so TMA issued sixty-four small requests instead of
four 256-byte ones. Widening the box moves no bytes and leaves the shared image identical.

The wider weight-scale box is applied to the flat route only. The fused SwiGLU route issues
two such streams per stage a fixed large stride apart, and there the same descriptor makes
the kernel 9% slower with nine times the run-to-run spread while its register and shared
usage stay identical -- contention that depends on addresses, not extra work. Measured, so
the fused route keeps its 16-byte rows; a comment next to the constant records why.

Measured on RTX 5090 (sm_120a), qwen3_8_27b_nvfp4, int8 KV, prefill chunk 4096, T=4096,
palindromic A B B A ordering:

  linear_add [5120, 17408]     589.8 -> 577.5 us   (0.977)
  linear_add [5120, 6144]      251.9 -> 245.8      (0.976)
  fused SwiGLU [34816, 5120]  1181.7 -> 1171.5     (0.991)

Per-kernel GPU time over a 32768-token prefill (nsys --cuda-graph-trace=node), which is the
instrument that resolves this: all nvfp4 kernels 948.5 -> 936.7 ms (0.987), total prefill
GPU time 4069.3 -> 4057.7 ms (0.997). The decode SwiGLU route and the quantizer are
unchanged to three digits. Prefill wall clock cannot resolve a change this size and is not
offered as evidence.

Greedy generation matches the base bit for bit on four prompts. Decode is untouched by
construction: the route needs at least 256 tokens.
