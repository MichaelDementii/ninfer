perf(nvfp4): register the fused TMA SwiGLU for every chunk width it accepts

resolve_route took the fused TMA SwiGLU at exactly T == 1024, although the kernel accepts any
T % 256 == 0 and the workspace recipe beside it was already written in terms of that constraint.
Every wider prefill chunk therefore fell into LinearW4A4Post, which materialises the 34816xT bf16
projection into the arena and reads it back through a separate silu_mul.

The route now takes the fused kernel whenever tokens >= 1024 and tokens % 256 == 0, and the
capacity query is widened to match: it sizes the fused path for the largest accepted width in the
interval rather than for 1024 alone.

The capacity does not grow anywhere. Enumerated over 4560 (min_tokens, max_tokens) intervals it is
never larger than before, is smaller in 963, and the argument validation is unchanged. Where it
shrinks it is usually by one token of the materialised projection, because last_baseline steps down
when max_tokens itself now resolves to the fused route; on an interval collapsed to a single
accepted width the materialising path becomes unreachable and the whole projection leaves, which is
544 MiB at 8192. The engine does not request such an interval today, so the reported workspace peak
is unchanged and the win is traffic rather than capacity.

The two routes are not bit-identical and cannot be: the fused epilogue has the f32 accumulator in
hand and rounds once, while the materialising one rounds gate and up to bf16 and multiplies the
rounded values. This does not introduce that difference, it moves which widths see it - stock
already answers differently at 1024 and at 8192, and after this both answer the way 1024 does.
tests/ops/linear_swiglu/test_nvfp4.cpp qualifies both routes against the FP64 oracle today, at
T = 49 and 128 for the materialising one and T = 1024 for the fused one, and this changes neither.
Extending its A4 case list to the widths that do change route, both arms pass the same criterion:
relative-L2 is 0.0513 against 0.0516 at 1280 and 0.0497 against 0.0501 at 2048, and equal at 4096,
where the gross ratio is the one figure that moves the other way, 0.326 against 0.286. Every cell
sits far inside a criterion the shipped widths already reach 0.639 of.
